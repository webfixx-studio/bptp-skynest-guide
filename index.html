Project Overview

BPTP SKYNEST Towers Sector 80, Greater Faridabad ka ek premium residential project hai jo luxury, comfort, aur modern lifestyle ko ek hi place par laata hai.

BPTP SKYNEST Towers ko Faridabad ke tallest residential landmarks mein se ek maana ja raha hai, jisme twin towers approx 150 metres tak rise karte hain. Project mein 3 BHK aur 4 BHK residences hain, aur reported unit sizes lagbhag 3,185 sq. ft. se 3,856 sq. ft. tak hain.

Is project ko ultra-luxury segment mein position kiya gaya hai, jahan spacious layouts, premium finishing, aur lifestyle-centric planning ko priority di gayi hai. RERA registration HRERA-PKL-FBD-881-2026 ke under listed hai.

Location Advantage

Project Sector 80, Greater Faridabad mein located hai, jo Delhi-NCR connectivity ke angle se kaafi strong belt maana jaata hai. Nearby connectivity points mein DND corridor, KMP side movement, aur upcoming infra links ka mention milta hai.

Location ka ek aur plus point ye hai ki project Omaxe World Street jaise popular area ke close bataya ja raha hai, jo daily convenience aur lifestyle access ko aur easy banata hai.

Luxury Design

SKYNEST ka concept sirf ek high-rise building ka nahi, balki vertical garden style premium living ka hai. Yeh biophilic design, wraparound balconies, aur open-air lifestyle feel ke saath planned hai.

Project mein only 4 apartments per core ka mention bhi milta hai, jo privacy aur exclusivity ko highlight karta hai.

Comfort and Amenities

Amenities ka scale kaafi strong hai, jisme clubhouse, swimming pool, gym, indoor games, jogging tracks, kids play area, meditation area, parking, security, aur landscaped garden jaise features include kiye gaye hain.

Kids, adults, aur senior citizens ke liye spaces ko thoughtfully plan kiya gaya hai, jo modern luxury housing ka strong sign hai.

Floor Plans and Sizes

Available information ke according 3 BHK aur 4 BHK configurations premium size range mein aate hain. Kuch listings mein 3 BHK around 3,185 sq. ft. aur 4 BHK around 3,856 sq. ft. diya gaya hai, jabki approximate pricing ₹4.45 crore se ₹5.40 crore ke beech hai.
